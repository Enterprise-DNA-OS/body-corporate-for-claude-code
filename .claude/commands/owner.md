description: One owner across the whole portfolio. Their lots, their arrears, their committee seats and the contact history.
---

1. Run `npm run strata -- owner <name>` (or `owners [word]` for the list).
2. Lead with what they own and what they owe. An investor with lots in two schemes is one person with one phone number; treat the relationship as one.
3. If they sit on a committee, say so before drafting anything to them. A chairperson gets a different letter than a first-time owner.
4. Before drafting any message to an owner, read this card and the contact log on it. Then draft to `drafts/` and stop. A person sends.

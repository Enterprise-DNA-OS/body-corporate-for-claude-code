description: One body corporate in full. The lots and owners, the committee, the levies, the meetings, the insurance, the maintenance and the compliance register, on one card.
---

1. Run `npm run strata -- scheme <code or name>`.
2. Present it the way a manager briefs a chairperson: the headline position first (arrears, AGM status, insurance, open work), then the detail.
3. Anything in capitals in the output (EXPIRED, OVERDUE, NOT SENT, NONE ON FILE) is a finding, not a formatting choice. Surface every one of them.
4. If the ask was a question ("is Harbour View insured", "when is the Millhouse AGM"), answer the question in the first line and attach the card after it.

Related look-ups: `lot <ref>` for one unit's levy file, `owner <name>` for one owner across schemes, `committee <scheme>` for the seats, `meetings --scheme=` for the meeting record.
